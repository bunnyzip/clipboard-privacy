Privacy Policy — ClipBoard

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · App: ClipBoard (com.bunnyzip.clipboard) · Developer: bunnyzip

This policy explains what data the ClipBoard Android application ("the App") handles, where it is stored, and what is never collected. We built the App so that all of your data stays on your own device.

1. What the App stores — and where

  • Clipboard text you copy. When the App is running and detects new text on your device's clipboard, it saves that text into a private, password-protected-at-the-OS-level database inside the App's own storage on your device (Android app-private storage, not accessible to other apps or to us).
  • Pin status and timestamps for each saved entry (used for the pin and auto-expiry features).
  • Credit balance (a number kept in the App's private storage).

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your clipboard content — or any other personal data. There is no server. There is no account.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, precise location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.
  • The App contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers.

3. Data retention and deletion

  • Unpinned entries are automatically and permanently deleted from your device 6 months after they were saved.
  • Pinned entries are kept until you unpin or delete them.
  • You can delete any entry at any time from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored — nothing survives on any server, because nothing ever left your device.

4. Clipboard access

The App reads the system clipboard only while its monitoring service is running, and only to save copies you choose to keep. Android may show a notice that ClipBoard read the clipboard; that reading happens entirely on your device. We have no visibility into it.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional in-app purchases of "credit" packs, processed entirely by Google Play. When you buy credits:

  • Payment details are handled by Google; the App never sees your payment information.
  • Purchase records (product ID and purchase token) are processed by Google Play to deliver and restore your purchase. No purchase data is tied to your identity by us.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the ongoing "ClipBoard is running" notification that keeps monitoring alive. You may deny it; monitoring may then be stopped by Android in the background.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to restart monitoring after a device reboot.
  • Foreground service: required by Android for the persistent monitoring notification.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone — child or adult.

8. Security

All user data is stored in Android's app-private storage, which the operating system sandboxes from other apps. Data is never transmitted over the network by the App (the only network component is Google Play's billing library during a purchase).
